This Privacy Policy explains how ColorWorks Vinyl ("we", "us") handles information on this website. ColorWorks Vinyl is based in Lake Zurich, IL.

What this website is for

This website shows what we make and how to contact us or request a quote. We do not take orders or payments on this website.

Information we collect

  • No forms. This website has no contact form and no account sign-up. You can reach us only by email or phone.
  • No cookies or analytics. This website does not set cookies and has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. You will not see a cookie notice. It does not load anything from other websites: fonts, icons and images come from this website.
  • Emails and calls. If you email or call us, we receive what you choose to send: your name, your contact details (such as your email address or phone number), your project details, and any files you send, such as a logo or artwork.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The GitHub Pages documentation says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub handles that data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to answer you, prepare a quote and fill your request. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Our email and phone providers process messages for us. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep it as long as needed to answer you and for normal business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Email info@colorworksvinyl.com or call 1.833.CWVINYL.

Do Not Track

This website does not track visitors, so browser Do Not Track signals do not change how it works.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
