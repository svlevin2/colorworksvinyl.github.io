This Privacy Policy explains how ColorWorks Vinyl ("we", "us") handles information on this website. ColorWorks Vinyl is based in Lake Zurich, IL.

What this website is for

This website shows what we make and how to contact us or request a quote. We do not take orders or payments on this website.

Information we collect

  • No forms. This website has no contact form and no account sign-up.
  • No cookies or trackers. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Emails and calls. If you email or call us, we receive your message, your contact details and anything you send, such as artwork.
  • Hosting logs. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to answer your question, prepare a quote or a digital proof, and follow up on your request. We do not sell your information. To confirm: we do not add people who email us to a mailing list unless they ask

Who can see it

Our email and phone providers process messages for us. We may share information if the law requires it.
