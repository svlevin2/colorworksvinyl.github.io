Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ColorWorks Vinyl wants everyone to be able to use this website.

What we checked

On November 3, 2026 we tested this website. These checks passed:

  • An automated accessibility scan (axe, WCAG 2.2 level A and AA rules) found 0 issues on every page. It covers items like image text descriptions, headings and link names.
  • The Lighthouse accessibility score was 100 on every page, on phone and desktop settings.
  • All 34 text and background color pairs meet the WCAG AA contrast level. The lowest ratio is 5.35 to 1, and AA asks for 4.5 to 1. Text over photos was checked in 33 places, and all passed.
  • With a keyboard on the home, storefront signage, FAQ and privacy pages, the first Tab press reaches a "Skip to content" link, every link and button shows a visible focus outline, and the Services menu and the phone menu both work. Escape closes the phone menu.
  • When your device asks for reduced motion, page content shows without animation.
  • On phone and tablet screen sizes from 320 to 1024 pixels wide, pages do not scroll sideways. On phone sizes, buttons and stand-alone links are at least 44 by 44 pixels and body text is at least 16 pixels. We tested this on every page with the Chromium, WebKit and Firefox browser engines.

Automated tests and browser engines do not catch every problem, so we welcome your feedback.
