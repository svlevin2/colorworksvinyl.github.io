ColorWorks Vinyl wants everyone to be able to use this website.

What we did

We built this website with accessibility in mind, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This includes:

  • text and background colors chosen for readable contrast
  • text descriptions (alt text) on images
  • one clear heading structure on each page
  • a skip link, keyboard access and a visible focus outline
  • buttons and links large enough to tap on a phone
  • less motion when your device asks for reduced motion
  • pages that work on phones, tablets and computers
